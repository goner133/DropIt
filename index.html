WHY

The beat is the grid. The accents are the edit.

Beat-sync tools put a cut on every beat. Tidy, and boring after ten seconds. The edits that stop a scroll land on the kick, the snare, the hit, the drop.

DROP IT! hears all of them, bass and hi-hats too, even a sound you pick by ear. You choose which ones get a cut.

WHO'S IN CHARGE

You're the editor.

DROP IT! doesn't make your edit for you. It does the listening and the counting, and hands you a first cut or a set of marks to work from.

Which shot, how long, what stays and what goes: that's still you. It just gets you to the fun part faster.

EVERYTHING IN THE PANEL

The full list.

Listen

Analyze music, about 10 s for a 3-minute track, cached per file
Tempo with half and double time
Lanes: beat, kick, bass, snare, hi-hat, perc, hits, chords, a custom band
Find this sound: any sound you mark becomes its own lane, inside Music
Song sections: intro, build, drop and more
Lamps synced to the playhead at 60 fps, solo per lane, click a mark to jump

Cut

SURPRISE ME! in Super calm, Calm, Balanced, Driving, Hype
Reads the music in phrases, holds shots on drops
Cuts a frame before the hit, or after it, your choice
Seed number replays any version exactly
Re-roll selected shots: new clips or new cuts
Empty slots to test a rhythm, then Fill slots

Bursts + FX

Bursts: Suggest finds where they fit, Auto lets SURPRISE ME! place them, or Burst here for a spot you choose
Flash frames on burst cuts: white, black, overexpose, invert, B&W, on their own track
FX: empty adjustment layers on drops, centred on or starting at the hit
An adjustment layer at the playhead
Coloured markers per kind of accent, on the sequence or the music clip

Fix

Check my edit: finds the nearest strong accent for each of your cuts
Snap: trims cuts onto it, never moves the picture inside a clip
One Undo for the whole snap

Work

Undo last action, 8 steps
Simple by default, Pro when you need it
Live sync follows Premiere, or switch it off
Panel size: Auto, or 100/90/80/70%
Keyboard keys
Tooltips on everything

Match (experimental)

Lines up a second song to the first one’s beat, bar or phrase
Suggests a switch point, or line it up at the playhead
Tested on a few songs so far: still rough around the edges

FAQ

Questions.

Is it really free?+

Yes. The beta is pay what you like, and $0 is fine.

What happens at 1.0?+

1.0 will be a paid release. Everyone who reviews the beta on Gumroad gets it free.

What can it hear?+

Beat, kick, snare, bass, hi-hat, percussion, hits, chords and song sections like the drop. Plus a custom frequency band, and any sound you mark by ear: it finds every repeat.

Does it work on a Mac?+

It hasn't been tested on a Mac yet. It might run, but I can't promise it. Windows is the safe choice for now.

Which Premiere Pro do I need?+

Premiere Pro 2026, version 26.0 or newer.

Will it mess up my project?+

It adds cuts, markers and layers to your sequence. Snap trims cuts and never moves the picture inside a clip. Every action is one Undo.

Does it need the internet?+

No. The analysis runs on your computer, and the plugin does not ask for network access.

How is it different from beat-sync plugins?+

Beat-sync tools cut on the beat grid. DROP IT! hears the accents inside the music, kicks, snares, hits, drops, your own sounds, and can build a whole first cut for you to change.

I found a bug. Where do I send it?+

Email pszmilyk@gmail.com with what you did, your Premiere version and the log from the panel: click the gear, press Copy log, and paste it into your email. The log shows me exactly where it broke.
