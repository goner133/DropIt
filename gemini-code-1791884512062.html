A native Adobe Premiere Pro panel that listens to the whole track so you don't have to cut event reels manually.

Why DROP IT!?

Cutting event recaps into short reels for social media eats hours. Tools like BeatEdit cut on every beat, which gets boring fast. The edits that actually hit are the ones landing on the kick in the drop, the snare fill before it, the vocal chop, or unique track sounds.

DROP IT! is a native Premiere Pro panel (UXP, Premiere 2026) built by an editor, for editors, to solve exactly this problem.

Key Features

  • Layered Audio Analysis: Hears the track in separate lanes for beat, kick, snare, hats, bass, chords, and big hits in a live view following your playhead.
  • Song Structure Awareness: Understands intro, build, drop, and break, telling you what to cut on in each section.
  • Find This Sound: Place the playhead on any sound, check its frequency band, and let the plugin find every instance (great for buried kicks, vocal chops, or risers).
  • SURPRISE ME! Cuts: Cuts your timeline, bin, or selection based on style (Calm, Balanced, Driving, Hype) with long holds on drops and short bursts before them. Every version has a seed so you can recover it.
  • Empty Slots & FX Layers: Lay out colored placeholders to judge rhythm before adding footage, and auto-place empty adjustment layers on peak moments.
  • Check & Snap: Cut by feel, hit Check, and pull near-misses onto real hits instantly with one-click undo.

System Requirements

  • Software: Adobe Premiere Pro 2026 (26.0 or newer) with developer mode enabled.
  • Platform: Windows Only (for now).
